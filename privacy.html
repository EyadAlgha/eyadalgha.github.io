Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who uses this app

personal-trainer is used only by its owner, Eyad Alghamdi. No other users are accepted.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent, the app reads the owner's own health data from the Google Health API: sleep sessions, daily steps and daily resting heart rate. Access is read-only. The app never writes to or deletes data in the owner's Google account.

How the data is used and stored

The data is copied into a private database in the owner's private GitHub repository, for the owner's personal fitness tracking. It is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
